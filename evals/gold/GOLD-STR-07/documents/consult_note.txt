SYNTHETIC RECORD - Example Northgate Rheumatology Consultants - Consultation Letter - 2026-06-15
Patient reference SYN-1120.

Dear Referring Physician,

Thank you for sending this 58-year-old man for rheumatology consultation. He has an established diagnosis of seropositive rheumatoid arthritis, made at this practice in 2021 based on positive anti-CCP antibodies and symmetric polyarthritis.

He completed a trial of oral methotrexate from November 3, 2025 to May 4, 2026, which was discontinued because his synovitis and pain did not improve despite dose titration. He works as a long-haul truck driver and reports that prolonged sitting worsens his hand stiffness. He has a remote history of a kidney stone but no other chronic conditions.

Given the inadequate response to methotrexate, I support proceeding with Immunara. The enclosed medication history documents this course.

Kind regards,
The consulting rheumatologist
